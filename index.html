Day 7 the fog starts lifting

Built for the moment the urge hits — not just the moment after.

Most blockers stop at blocking. Blockd is designed around what recovery actually feels like: the trigger, the urge, the wobble, the win.

🛡️

On-device content filter

Adult content is blocked at the network level, entirely on your iPhone. No accounts to bypass it in a weak moment, no browsing data sent anywhere.

🌊

Urge support that talks you through it

When an urge hits, Blockd walks you through it — naming the trigger, riding the intensity, and reflecting after. It learns your patterns and meets you there.

🧠

Watch your brain recover

A living, animated brain heals as you do — through withdrawal, rewiring, and beyond. Progress you can see makes progress you can keep.

📓

Journal & lessons

Short, honest lessons on how compulsion works and daily journaling to process it — built like a path, not a lecture.

🫂

Community that gets it

Panic button

Day 30 this stays between you and you

Recovery is personal. So is your data.

Blockd is local-first by architecture, not by promise. The sensitive parts of your recovery physically never leave your phone.

Your journal, urges, and streaks are stored on your device — we run no server that holds them.
The filter runs on-device. We never see, log, or transmit your browsing.
No ads, no trackers, no advertising SDKs, no data brokers. Ever.
The community is anonymous — no real names, and you can delete your posts anytime.

Why does iOS show VPN when Blockd is on? Apple only lets apps filter content system-wide through its VPN framework. Blockd uses it to run the filter on your iPhone. Your traffic never goes through our servers, and Blockd doesn't hide your IP or change your location. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
